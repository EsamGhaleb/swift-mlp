[Gesture coding]
Manual gestures for the following 15 pairs were first automatically segmented by a co-speech gesture detection model by Ghaleb et al. (2024) and then fixed by research assistants if necessary (see "gesture_coding_scheme_v2.docx" for details).

One important difference between manually segmented gestures and automatically segmented gestures is that while manual segmentation only annotated the stoke phase of gestures, automatic segmentation included some parts of preparation and retraction.
